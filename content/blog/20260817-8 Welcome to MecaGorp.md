Here we go, a brand-new website for MecaGorp! This will hopefully be a central nexus of information for [Chorus](/chorus/) and its related projects.

There isn't a whole lot here yet aside from the [current Chorus specification](/chorus/latest) but hopefully there will be more stuff to share eventually.

.....

Here is the current state of things:

* [Chorus](/chorus/) has recently been updated to [v0.3.2](5), which adds the following functionality:

    * Structured tags
    * Long descriptions

* [Sockpuppet](https://sockpuppet.band/) publishes a Chorus 0.3.x feed, ready to be tested against by new consumer implementations
* [Fairplayer](https://fairplayer.band/) supports the older/deprecated 0.2 specification (which was called "Canimus") as a receiver; as such, [Sockpuppet is available there](https://fairplayer.band/player/artist/sockpuppet@sockpuppet.band)
* [Mirlo](https://mirlo.space/) allows artists to opt into distributing music via a 0.2.x feed as well; so for example this is how [Godspeed You! Black Emperor](https://mirlo.space/godspeed-you-black-emperor) is [available on Fairplayer](https://fairplayer.band/player/artist/godspeed-you-black-emperor@mirlo.space), as are many, many other Mirlo artists
