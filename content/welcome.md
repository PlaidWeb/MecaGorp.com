Welcome to MecaGorp, a website for information about the [Chorus protocol](/chorus/) and the overall design of an independent, artist-focused streaming platform which is controlled by people, not corporations.

For more information, please look at the following website sections:

* [Chorus](/chorus/): The current specification for the interoperability protocol
* [Whitepapers](/whitepapers/): Technical discussions about how the various pieces may work together
* [Blog](/blog/): For updates on this project and its related efforts

### About MecaGorp

The name is a [spoonerism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spoonerism) of "megacorp" and emerged from a joke in one of the Chorus example files. It's meant to poke fun at the idea that the music industry is full of massive megacorporations that do not care about the interests of its creatives or customers. It is not any sort of actual corporation, mega-, micro- or otherwise.
